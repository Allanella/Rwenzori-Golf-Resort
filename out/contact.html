Directions

Getting There

By Air

Fly into Entebbe International Airport or land directly at the Kasese Airstrip. We offer arranged airport pickups and private transfers directly to the resort.

By Road

Easily accessible via Kampala–Fort Portal highway. Turn off just 4km before reaching Fort Portal on the main Fort Portal–Kasese road. We are only a brief, 20-minute drive from Fort Portal City.

Private Transfer

We arrange private luxury 4x4 vehicles from Kampala, Entebbe, or Fort Portal City center directly to our gates 4km off the main Kasese highway.

Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

01
What is the best time to visit Mt. Rwenzori?

Mt. Rwenzori is beautiful year-round. The dry seasons (June–July and December–February) offer the clearest weather and best golf conditions.

02
Do I need to book in advance?

We recommend booking 2–4 weeks ahead, especially during peak seasons. Last-minute bookings are subject to availability.

03
What amenities are included with accommodation?

All rooms include breakfast, WiFi, access to all facilities (golf, spa, terrace), and 24/7 concierge service.

04
Can I bring my family with children?

Absolutely! Mt. Rwenzori is family-friendly with activities suitable for all ages. Contact us for family packages.

05
What is your cancellation policy?

Cancellations made 14 days before arrival are fully refundable. Within 14 days, a 50% fee applies.

06
Are pets allowed?

Yes, well-behaved pets are welcome. Please notify us in advance — a small pet fee applies.
